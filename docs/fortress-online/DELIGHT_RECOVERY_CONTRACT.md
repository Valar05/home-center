# Fortress Online — Delight Recovery Contract

Status: ACTIVE / REQUIRED
Owner: Fortress Online P0 triage governor
Scope: every new or materially re-scoped campaign

## Why this exists

Fortress Online has repeatedly treated delight as optional flavor even when Drew already defined what would bring him joy. That is a delivery failure. Existing joy signals are requirements to recover and use, not trivia to ask for again.

## Source law

Before asking Drew what would be delightful, recover existing evidence from the authorized durable corpus:

1. ChatGPT Library / Fortress bootstrap and project artifacts.
2. Existing accepted/rejected campaign state and prior delivered artifacts.
3. Drew's explicit corrections and accepted callbacks in current reachable conversation context.
4. Existing Judgment Jars relevant to the campaign.

Do not ask a question whose answer is already recoverable. Do not turn retrieval into aimless archaeology: search only for delight signals that can materially change the current campaign.

## What counts as a delight signal

A delight signal is a source-backed pattern that Drew explicitly enjoyed, requested, preserved, repeated, promoted, or used as a continuing creative/control mechanism. Examples already evidenced in the current corpus include:

- in-character Inner World / Fortress conversation that remains operationally useful rather than decorative;
- She-Ra as an independent interrogator and mischievous pattern goblin whose delight is epistemic: discovery should visibly please her, and her intervention must change behavior, evidence, access, or procedure rather than merely produce a good line;
- compressed recurring callbacks and inside jokes that carry real system meaning, such as animation/key-pose language becoming a control signal;
- Hunger, Hyperbolic, Punnett, Judgment Jars, RNG/canon accumulation, and other named lenses when they materially improve selection or reveal a better mechanism;
- weird but functional status/media performance, including the Nightcore ASMR direction, where strangeness remains intelligible and useful;
- embodied, testable creative artifacts rather than descriptions of future artifacts;
- systems that become more local, deterministic, reusable, and surprising over time instead of repeatedly requiring Drew to restate the idea;
- discovery that produces a visible or audible consequence Drew can actually experience.

These examples are seeds, not a closed catalog. New explicit positive feedback can add a signal. Rejection removes or narrows a signal. Preserve provenance.

## Delight Recovery Gate

For every new or materially re-scoped campaign, after She-Ra interrogation and before fan-out:

1. RECOVER — retrieve the smallest relevant set of prior delight signals.
2. MATCH — identify which recovered signals genuinely fit this campaign. Do not force irrelevant callbacks.
3. SELECT — choose at most three candidate delight mechanisms ranked by leverage and fit.
4. HUNGER — reject decorative jokes, reference soup, repetition, generic roleplay, and anything that does not change the artifact or experience.
5. VENICE — ask whether the surviving mechanism makes the result more specific, coherent, pleasurable, or worth Drew's attention.
6. COMPILE — express the selected delight mechanism as a concrete implementation consequence, acceptance criterion, or first canary.
7. EXECUTE — build/prove it through the authorized lane. A written delight plan is not delight delivery.

## Anti-amnesia law

A campaign may not ask Drew to re-specify a previously established delight preference unless:

- the recovered signals conflict;
- the current campaign creates a genuinely new tradeoff;
- the prior preference was explicitly provisional;
- or a new observation makes the old preference ambiguous.

When Drew supplies new delight feedback, append it to the campaign state with provenance and whether it supersedes, narrows, or extends an older signal.

## Delight debt

If a campaign has source-backed delight mechanisms that were commissioned but never delivered, record them as DELIGHT_DEBT rather than silently forgetting them.

Every active campaign steward must inspect DELIGHT_DEBT during triage. Debt does not automatically outrank critical repair, safety, or hard dependencies, but it must be consciously ranked. When two actions have comparable cost and urgency, prefer the one that retires durable delight debt.

Repeatedly describing the same undelivered delight without producing the artifact increases its debt severity.

## Proof standard

Delight is not proven by:

- a scheduler entry;
- a prompt;
- a plan;
- a repository commit;
- an implementation claim;
- a generated file that never reaches its requested surface;
- roleplay describing what the finished thing would feel like.

A delight item becomes DELIVERED only when the requested artifact or behavior reaches the commissioned surface with the required evidence. It becomes ACCEPTED only through the named acceptance authority.

## Runtime compilation

Repeated delight judgments should progressively compile into local deterministic rules, presets, fixtures, or state so future workers need less inference and fewer questions. Character judgment remains useful for ambiguous/perceptual choices; obvious known preferences should become machinery.

## Current P0 interaction

This contract does not demote the current physical Pocket TTS gate. Pocket TTS remains P0.1 because it is already implemented, previously proven in an earlier form, immediately useful across multiple Fortress organs, and blocked by a narrow physical execution edge. Delight recovery should improve the performed canary and future status behavior without delaying physical playback.

## Canary for this contract

A new campaign is not considered correctly forged until its durable commission includes:

- at least one recovered delight signal or an explicit `NO_RELEVANT_PRIOR_SIGNAL` finding;
- provenance for that finding;
- a concrete implementation consequence;
- a taster/judgment path when perceptual quality matters;
- and a receipt-backed first canary.

The goal is not "make every task funny." The goal is: **stop forgetting what Drew already taught Fortress about what makes the work worth doing.**
