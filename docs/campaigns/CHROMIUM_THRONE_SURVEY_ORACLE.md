# Chromium Throne Survey Oracle

**Status:** COMMISSIONED / RUNTIME UNPROVEN
**Owner:** Drew
**Priority:** TODAY

## Outcome

Complete one real paid survey through the existing Chromium Throne on THECAULDRON while Drew controls only the irreducible human answers from the phone.

## Mission boundary

Preserve the existing architecture. Do not substitute Browser Nerve, Computer Hands, a phone browser, a new browser farm, or a new control plane for Chromium Throne.

Runtime topology:

```text
Drew phone
  -> existing Home Center / phone-facing control surface
  -> Chromium Throne relay
  -> dedicated authenticated Chromium profile on THECAULDRON
  -> real survey site
```

THECAULDRON owns browser execution. The phone is Drew's interrupt/approval surface.

## Human bandwidth law

Do not ask Drew to regenerate Drew.

For each survey field:

1. Deterministic navigation / non-subjective mechanics: Throne handles it.
2. Stable factual answer already explicitly established by Drew: retrieve evidence and draft from it.
3. Open-ended answer supported by Drew-authored corpus: retrieve primary-source Drew material and compress it into the requested answer length.
4. Missing or genuinely novel subjective information: bubble only the smallest semantic question to Drew's phone.
5. Drew may answer with the minimum useful payload, including one word or a short phrase.
6. Expand only what Drew's current answer plus retrieved Drew-authored evidence supports.
7. Show the final answer to Drew for approval before submitting an open-ended subjective response.
8. Submit only after approval.

Evidence hierarchy:

```text
Drew's current answer
> Drew's explicit prior first-person statements
> stable factual profile explicitly established by Drew
> faithful linguistic expansion
> inference
```

Inference never silently becomes autobiography.

## Survey integrity

Never fabricate personal facts, opinions, experiences, demographics, product use, purchases, employment, household state, or preferences to qualify for a survey.

Never optimize an answer toward a presumed screener target.

If evidence is absent or conflicting, bubble the missing question to Drew.

Respect survey/site terms and stop if the site prohibits the automation being attempted.

## Phone bubble contract

The phone should request only the irreducible human delta, for example:

```text
SURVEY NEEDS DREW
Why did you dislike it?
> boring
```

The expanded candidate then returns:

```text
PROPOSED ANSWER
I found the experience repetitive and it did not hold my attention for very long. After a while, continuing felt more tedious than rewarding.

[APPROVE] [CORRECT]
```

Do not require Drew to type the survey question, copy browser text, or manually shuttle expanded prose.

## Retrieval contract

Prefer primary Drew-authored corpus in Drive/Project context when relevant. Retrieval must be question-scoped, not a grand corpus scan.

Every generated open-ended answer must be internally attributable to:

- current Drew input; and/or
- retrieved Drew-authored evidence.

If neither supports the answer, ask Drew.

## One-survey canary

Acceptance requires all of the following on one real survey:

1. Survey is visibly open in the dedicated Chromium Throne profile on THECAULDRON.
2. Throne advances at least one mechanical/non-subjective step without Drew touching the desktop.
3. At least one genuine subjective/open-ended question is detected.
4. That question or its smallest semantic delta reaches Drew's phone automatically.
5. Drew supplies a minimal answer from the phone.
6. System expands it faithfully using only current Drew input and/or retrieved Drew-authored evidence.
7. Final proposed answer is shown on the phone.
8. Drew approves or corrects it.
9. Approved answer is entered into the survey on THECAULDRON without Drew touching the desktop.
10. Survey advances to the next page/question.
11. Durable receipt records site/survey identity where allowed, timestamps, question classification, provenance class used for the answer, approval, and resulting page transition without storing unnecessary sensitive answer content.
