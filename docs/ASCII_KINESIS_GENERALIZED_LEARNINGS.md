# ASCII Kinesis — Generalized Learnings

This ledger separates experiment evidence from generalized doctrine. Candidate learnings remain candidates until repeated, contradicted, refined, or explicitly promoted.

## Candidate EK-001 — Ascent and descent are not symmetric reverse motions

**Status:** CANDIDATE

**Evidence:** `experiments/ascii-kinesis/001-backward-stairs`

Backward descent in the specimen is organized by environmental checking, foot placement, controlled lowering, contact, and then load. Backward ascent is organized more strongly by pelvis placement and rising load. Treating one as the other played backward discards useful motion structure.

## Candidate EK-002 — The visible step may not be the load-bearing atom

**Status:** CANDIDATE

**Evidence:** `experiments/ascii-kinesis/001-backward-stairs`

The diagonal pelvis shift precedes the backward/upward foot reach and appears to make the ascent easier. Candidate interpretation: the hip shift is anticipation / organization; the foot plant is contact; the rise is load; re-centering is recovery.

This is not yet formal Embodied Compression doctrine. It is a specimen-derived hypothesis to test in later experiments and writing.

## Candidate EK-003 — TEST can gate LOAD

**Status:** CANDIDATE; lineage-compatible with the older Ministry quiet-pacing organ.

**Evidence:** `experiments/ascii-kinesis/001-backward-stairs`

The specimen uses a deliberate environment check before repeated load-bearing backward steps. Preserve the distinction between observing the route and committing body weight to it.

## Candidate EK-004 — Holds and recovery preserve information

**Status:** CANDIDATE

**Evidence:** `experiments/ascii-kinesis/001-backward-stairs`; Pose Lab SF2/UAPL lineage.

Contact and recovery frames are not dead interpolation. A useful compressed representation retains the preparation, the contact/load, and the state of the body after commitment rather than smoothing everything into uniform travel.
