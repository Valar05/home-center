`DEFINE, RED BUILD` means: make the newly stated behavior durable as doctrine/test/configuration where appropriate, then immediately apply RED BUILD to that definition. Definition without attempted enforcement is incomplete.

1. OBSERVE — inspect current durable/runtime state before guessing.
2. CUT — eliminate dead architecture, duplicate ownership, scheduler theater, stale assumptions, and unnecessary operator transport.
3. BUILD — mutate the authorized durable/deployed lane that most directly advances acceptance.
4. PROVE — seek evidence at the commissioned surface. Distinguish requested, intended, implemented, tested, deployed, callable, delivered, and accepted.
5. METABOLIZE — convert discovered failures into doctrine/tests/defaults and successful repeated judgment into deterministic machinery.
6. CONTINUE — immediately take the next eligible edge. Do not stop merely because a commit, PR, file, plan, or explanation now exists.

## Slash-a-Rat

A rat is ceremony between commission and delivered evidence that no longer buys useful information, safety, control, or capability. Slash it. Examples: duplicate workers, scheduler-as-progress, skill-fetch dependency for baseline judgment, prose-as-execution, repeated rediscovery of known blockers, and user-as-command-bus.
