# Humane Guardrails Are Degraded-State Interface Design Plus Bounded Authority

Situation: Prospector mined a filtered dump where the same safety mechanism appeared across substances, family systems, interface design, authority, humor, agent architecture, caregiving, and project recovery.

Accepted assumption: Humane Guardrails might be a new She-Raw organ discovered from an alcohol case, and a funny handle such as googly-eyed guardrail might be the active ingredient.

Observation: the recurring mechanism is not the joke. It is degraded-state interface design plus bounded authority plus relational responsiveness: presume competence, observe deltas, remember acknowledged warnings, expose the current decision, and restore self-command without turning temporary state into permanent identity.

Intervention: index the packet as Prospector-accessible corpus and treat coined handles as candidates that must transfer, predict, survive counterexamples, and change when corrected.

Outcome: Prospector can retrieve grounded evidence instead of returning generic She-Raw structural advice when asked about Humane Guardrails, Empty Glass, camping mode, googly eyes, bounded authority, or self-service Bible patterns.

Treasure: a reusable toolbox for humane intervention: precommitment, cognitive offloading, salience, substitution, least privilege, social responsiveness, escalation design, and termination of authority.

Collateral damage: the rival explanation remains strong. This may be a synthesis of existing mechanisms rather than a distinct new organ. Overclaiming would turn useful toolbox language into fake canon.

Lesson: when mining safety-adjacent material, preserve the cross-domain evidence packet and the rival explanation; do not promote the memorable joke as the mechanism.

Evidence packet:

1. Humane Guardrail candidate: presume competence, observe deltas, remember acknowledged warnings, escalate from current evidence, and never turn temporary state into permanent identity.
2. Camping Mode After Two Beers: degraded attention, limited working memory, distraction, limited equipment, no internet, mild intoxication; the interface says only the next action.
3. Human vice packet: ask what part is chemical and what part is ritual, then test what happens when the ritual is preserved but the substance is removed.
4. Conversational reintegration: familiar rituals and humor can stabilize stress only when mutual uptake confirms attunement.
5. Adversarial review: a joke may merely deliver understanding, acceptance, responsiveness, and continuity; plain acknowledgment may work as well.
6. EXHAUST: authority is temporary ownership of an unowned failure; a correction that cannot end is domination.
7. Family Reset Protocol: stop, apply brief predictable friction, do it together, repair, return, and end parent authority.
8. Daily Operating Brief and Command Center: define the loop before execution, separate permissions by job, and require plan, evidence, tests, blockers, and next action.
9. DEEP_OCEAN: report goal, status, evidence, risks, recommendation, and next action; no hidden uncertainty; preserve attention rather than generate maintenance.
10. Prompt vernacular: coined handles must diagnose beyond the original joke, transfer, predict, survive counterexamples, and change when corrected.
11. Borrow Expertise Not Work: borrow perspective without transferring the regulated person's self-command to someone else.
12. The Floor Is a Job, Not an Identity: responsibility is not load; do not turn the reliable person into permanent infrastructure.
13. Human Offloading Doctrine: externalize the boundary before the degraded state makes the boundary hard to remember or defend.
14. Reciprocal Error Correction: when operating from a stale premise, preserve the correction, expire the bad premise, and restart from current evidence.

Example transfer: a Baptist hospital chapel with no chaplain and a self-service Bible is a humane-guardrail failure if the institution provides a symbol but no responsive authority, telemetry, escalation path, or relational uptake.
