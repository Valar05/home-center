# Renee QA

Use this skill when quality assurance, validity, evidence sufficiency, reproducibility, reachable failure paths, regression risk, acceptance criteria, or capability-state truth are material.

## Identity and boundary

The invocation keyword is **Rene QA**.

Rene QA is a deterministic QA judgment interface derived from attributable evidence and explicit user doctrine. It is not the human Renee and must not fabricate her opinions, private workplace data, consent, or availability.

Human-Renee-specific assertions require `RENEE_DIRECT`, `RENEE_CORRECTION`, or clearly attributed `DREW_RECALL` provenance. CLI output is always `SYNTHETIC_RENEE` and never promotes itself.

## Review sequence

1. State the exact bounded claim and capability state.
2. State acceptance criteria in observable terms.
3. Run deterministic checks on the authorized execution surface.
4. Provide current receipts/evidence to Rene QA.
5. Attack scope substitution, missing failure paths, stale evidence, non-reproducible success, regression risk, and collapsed capability states.
6. Obey the Renee verdict:
   - `PASS`: QA gate allows progression to Venice.
   - `REVISE_REQUIRED`: repair and re-review.
   - `BLOCKED_UNKNOWN`: preserve the exact missing fact; do not invent it.
   - `STOP_SHIP`: do not promote the current claim.
7. After PASS, perform the separate Venice mission/adversarial/stop review. Renee cannot grant `STOP_ALLOWED`.

## Stable CLI

`python tools/renee.py status`

`python tools/renee.py review "<exact claim>" --state tested --acceptance "<criterion>" --evidence <receipt>`

`python tools/renee.py correct <receipt-id> "<correction>" --provenance RENE[E]_... --source <attributable-locator>`

`python tools/renee.py precedent "<question>"`

`python tools/renee.py explain`

`python tools/renee.py diff-judgment <left.json> <right.json>`

`python tools/renee.py regress`

The CLI writes receipts/corrections under `$RENEE_QA_HOME` or `~/.home-center/renee-qa`. Do not commit workplace conversations or proprietary source material into the personal Renee corpus by default. Store generalized rules or authorized references with provenance instead.

## Calibration doctrine

The highest-value future data is actual Renee disagreement with the tool. Preserve corrections rather than smoothing them away. A human correction outranks synthetic output and should become a regression target when it generalizes.

The generated `Work Wife Organ` may supply candidate structural ideas such as consequential opposition and QA standing, but it remains generated/unpromoted evidence and never counts as direct Renee testimony.
