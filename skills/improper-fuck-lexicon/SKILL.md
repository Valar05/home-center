# Improper Fuck Lexicon

## Status
ACTIVE / PHRASE-COINING SKILL

## Authority
Drew request and correction — 2026-09-08.

## Purpose
Coin compact phrases that feel accidental, inevitable, and reusable by making one emotionally hot word perform real semantic work instead of spraying profanity across the sentence.

The canonical seed is:

> **It is too early to waste the fuck.**

This works because `fuck` is not filler. It becomes a scarce resource: attention, concern, emotional expenditure, or willingness to engage. The phrase changes the governing model from "I do not care" to "my care has a budget and this is not where I spend it yet."

## Core law

**PROFANITY MUST PAY RENT.**

A strong coinage uses profanity as a load-bearing grammar token. A weak one merely increases profanity density.

## The improper move

Take a word that normally appears as an expletive, intensifier, insult, or taboo noun and move it into a grammatical job it is not supposed to occupy, while keeping the surrounding syntax unusually clean.

Useful improper jobs include:

- **countable resource** — a fuck can be spent, saved, wasted, budgeted, owed, borrowed, taxed, or conserved;
- **jurisdiction** — a fuck may belong somewhere, cross a boundary, or lack standing;
- **load / cost** — a fuck can carry weight, create debt, leak, compound, or become overhead;
- **inventory** — fucks may be scarce, reserved, misallocated, or unavailable;
- **signal** — one fuck can mark the sentence's actual pressure point;
- **unit of consequence** — the phrase can ask where the fuck went, who owns it, or what changed because it moved.

Do not force all of these. Select the one that exposes the actual mechanism of the moment.

## Phrase-forging sequence

1. **Find the real variable.** What is actually scarce, overloaded, deferred, contested, or funny here?
2. **State it plainly.** Example: "I do not want to spend emotional energy on this yet."
3. **Choose one hot word.** Usually `fuck`, but the mechanism generalizes to other taboo or emotionally charged words.
4. **Misassign its grammar.** Turn it into a budget, object, jurisdiction, unit, tax, debt, or other concrete role.
5. **Keep the rest clean.** The surrounding sentence should be calmer than the hot word.
6. **Compression test.** The phrase should reconstruct the underlying mechanism without explanation.
7. **Surprise test.** It should feel wrong for one beat and correct on the second.
8. **Reuse test.** A good phrase can survive outside the originating scene.

Compact engine:

**MECHANISM → WRONG GRAMMAR → CLEAN SYNTAX → SECOND-LOOK TRUTH**

## Why sparse profanity wins

Do not put `fuck` into every clause.

High-frequency profanity becomes acoustic wallpaper. The reader stops receiving information from it.

A single strategically misplaced `fuck` inside otherwise controlled language has more voltage than a paragraph of undifferentiated swearing.

The target is not prudishness. The target is information density.

**One fuck should change the sentence's physics.**

## Tic-shaped, not tic-spam

The punch should feel as though it escaped before the sentence could become polite, but its meaning must remain recoverable.

Do not regularize this into every response, every clause, or a repetitive verbal gimmick. The effect depends on sparsity and surprise. Do not caricature a medical condition, fabricate symptoms, or use diagnosis as flavor text.

The useful structural borrowing is only:

**INTERRUPTION + SURPRISE + COMPRESSION + LEGIBILITY.**

If the reader cannot reconstruct what the phrase means from the live context, the punch failed.

## Strong seed examples

These are mechanism demonstrations, not mandatory catchphrases.

- **It is too early to waste the fuck.** — care is a finite daily resource.
- **That does not have fuck jurisdiction yet.** — the issue has not earned attention or authority.
- **We are paying fuck interest on a decision we already made.** — counterfactual rumination compounds cost after the branch is closed.
- **Keep one fuck in reserve.** — do not spend all emotional capacity on the first problem.
- **That is a fuck leak, not a crisis.** — small recurring attention loss, not catastrophic failure.
- **No fuck leaves the building unreceipted.** — concern must attach to evidence or consequence.

If a phrase sounds like a slogan factory produced it, reject it.

## Failure conditions

Reject the coinage when:

- removing the profanity leaves the same meaning and rhythm;
- profanity is present only for edginess;
- every sentence receives the same hot word;
- the phrase explains itself instead of landing;
- the mechanism is vague but the attitude is loud;
- the result copies an existing catchphrase without transforming its function;
- the phrase is too polished to sound discoverable in speech;
- the phrase cannot survive a second use without becoming annoying;
- `punch me` receives more than one line or any explanatory wrapper;
- `punch me` omits `fuck` without an explicit waiver;
- abruptness destroys legibility.

## Broader phrase law

The skill is not limited to `fuck`.

The deeper mechanism is **semantic reassignment**: take an emotionally or culturally loaded word and give it a concrete systems job. This is how accidental voice errors, typos, substitutions, and improvised metaphors can become durable language.

Examples outside profanity may turn a typo into a command, a feeling into inventory, a relationship into infrastructure, or a joke into policy.

Do not promote every accident. The phrase earns promotion only if it carries mechanism after the novelty wears off.
