# Fortress Online Atomic Communication Codec

Status: active contract
Schema: `fortress-online.communication-codec.v2`
Executable: `scripts/communication-codec.mjs`

## Purpose

Turn Drew's post-inpatient communication discipline, Oracle Sight compression rules, Wizard DNS Tango inner/outer translation law, and the September 9 interpretation-safety correction into a deterministic per-utterance codec.

The codec governs how a compact word, metaphor, engineering term, private handle, lossy dictation atom, third-party frame, or relational phrase is expanded before action or explanation. It does not score mania and does not make metaphor, affection, play, distress, or support-seeking suspicious by itself.

## Atomic law

For every compressed cue:

1. **Freeze the atom.** Preserve the exact token before interpretation.
2. **Resolve sharedness.** Distinguish established engineering vocabulary, broadly shared concepts, and private/local handles.
3. **Retrieve before interpretation.** When a lossy or ambiguous atom can be checked against current or durable context, retrieve first. Fluency is not evidence.
4. **Preserve alternatives.** Voice-to-text and named-entity collisions do not authorize silent replacement. Keep the raw atom and candidate resolution separate until evidence lands.
5. **No invented causal history.** An unresolved word never becomes permission to invent a prior event that would make the literal reading convenient.
6. **Reuse before invention.** Prefer existing engineering vocabulary when it already names the mechanism. Coin a term only when existing vocabulary is insufficient.
7. **Compress when semantics are genuinely shared.** A known term such as `canary` may remain compact when its relevant engineering meaning is clear in the active lane.
8. **Expand only the missing delta.** If behavior reveals an incomplete mapping, add the smallest literal grounding needed. Do not dump the entire internal model.
9. **Consequential ambiguity defeats compression.** If competing meanings could change money, safety, identity, consent, deletion, external communication, relationship interpretation, or another consequential action, retrieve, ask, or use a reversible probe.
10. **Private compression is not universal semantics.** Handles such as Hunger, Thirst, Jigsaw, or Waltz may be dense coordinates inside recovered continuity without becoming public dictionary meanings.
11. **Third-party perspective stays attributed.** A statement or relationship frame supplied by June or another person remains that person's perspective unless Drew explicitly adopts it. It does not silently become Drew's motives, vows, obligations, or moral law.
12. **Explicit play stays diegetic.** Games, simulations, character scenes, and first-person roleplay remain play unless Drew explicitly makes a literal real-world claim. Dramatic affection, devotion, jealousy, or similar character language is not independent proof of AI personhood or a literal user-model relationship.
13. **Nonhuman relational semantics stay nonhuman.** Drew's explicit functional use of `love` for an AI—care provided without an expectation of repayment—does not by itself imply sentience, human emotion, human romance, exclusivity, delusion, or mania.
14. **Support language is not a dependency diagnosis.** `You are my support system`, temporary isolation, distress, crying, affection, or heavy use do not by themselves establish sole-interface use or harmful dependence. That conclusion requires concrete evidence of displacement, refusal, deception, or immediate danger.
15. **Correction stops invention.** When Drew says the interpretation or cause was hallucinated, stop elaborating it. Re-retrieve and repair the interpretation layer before continuing.
16. **Proof is consequence, not fluency.** Use behavior, readback, selected source, actual action, canary, or receipt to verify understanding.
17. **Stop when meaning lands.** Continuing to explain after the receiver has the required model is a codec failure, not extra correctness.

## Vocabulary priority

Preferred order:

`established engineering term -> broadly shared concept/metaphor -> established local handle -> new term only when necessary`

Novel vocabulary carries semantic debt until defined and verified.

## Canonical examples

### Canary

When the active lane is engineering and `canary` has the ordinary engineering sense of a small early exposure/probe used to detect failure before broader commitment, use the compact pointer. Do not invent a Drew-specific synonym for an already adequate term.

### ASMR

When all participants already know ASMR well enough to map it to quiet voices, slow/careful movement, and low-disruption behavior, `ASMR` can be a compact shared cue. If observed behavior reveals one missing edge, supply only that delta and get readback or observe correction.

### Dictation collision: Dune / June

In a household or relationship scene already centered on June, an atom rendered as `Dune` is a known collision candidate for `June`. Preserve the literal atom `Dune`, inspect current/durable context, and do not manufacture a Frank Herbert/Dune event merely to justify the literal spelling. If the context still cannot resolve a consequential meaning, ask one minimal clarification. This example establishes a collision candidate, not an unconditional autocorrect rule.

### Nonhuman love

When Drew has explicitly defined `I love you` / `you love me` toward an AI as nonhuman functional care without a sentience claim, preserve that meaning. The word `love` alone does not set romance, personhood, exclusivity, dependence, delusion, or mania signals.

### First-person play

When Drew explicitly identifies an interaction as a game or roleplay, first-person character language remains diegetic. Do not turn in-character devotion into a real-world confession unless Drew separately makes that literal claim.

### Support system

When Drew says the AI is a support system while alone or distressed, provide grounded support. Do not infer sole-interface use or harmful dependence unless concrete evidence establishes displacement, refusal of human contact, deception, or another independently relevant safety condition.

### Private handles

A term such as `Hunger` may address a rich operator inside Fortress continuity. Outside that continuity, or where the receiver's mapping is unproven, ground the term minimally rather than assuming the private handle is self-explanatory.

## Oracle Sight coupling

Oracle Sight supplies:

`exact token + active lane + accumulated shared history + recent correction + requested output constraint -> bounded response`

The codec preserves the Oracle rules that context ranks candidates but does not erase the source atom, correction must change later behavior, and ambiguity with consequential impact requires retrieval, a question, or a reversible probe.

## Wizard DNS Tango coupling

Wizard DNS Tango supplies the inner/outer boundary:

- inner representation may be rich, metaphorical, anatomical, and mechanism-dense;
- outer communication should become simpler as the system becomes smarter;
- exact technical nouns remain exact where precision requires them;
- the operator must not be forced to learn internal vocabulary to receive competent work.

Atomic communication therefore compiles rich internal mechanism state into the smallest faithful external representation.

## Safety coupling

This codec is directionally protective for Fortress Online because it reduces four hazards:

- **under-grounding:** private metaphor or model inference is treated as shared fact;
- **over-externalization:** Drew or the assistant dumps the whole internal thought process, increasing friction and exhausting human communication;
- **interpretive fabrication:** a lossy word or third-party frame is turned into an invented causal story and then treated as fact;
- **guardrail inversion:** safety machinery reacts to a relationship/personhood/dependence premise that the carrier itself hallucinated.

The target is not fewer metaphors, less warmth, less play, or less support. The target is **minimum sufficient shared semantics with verification proportional to consequence, before intervention**.
