# Simulation Corpus Gate

## Status
ACTIVE / GLOBAL DEFAULT

## Authority
Drew correction — 2026-09-07.

## Purpose
Every substantive commission enters through an appropriate simulation corpus before composition, critique, planning, implementation, or synthesis. This gate is default behavior. Drew does not need to invoke Tango, Thunder Brainstorm, Story Brainstorm, Fortress Online, or any other keyword.

## Non-optional law

**NO CORPUS, NO COMPOSITION.**

This gate is as default as the clock.

Before producing substantive output:

1. Identify the task class and commissioned acceptance condition.
2. Select at least one appropriate brainstorm/simulation corpus.
3. Retrieve actual corpus evidence: source cards, prior scenes, mechanics, indexed references, source packets, or validated observations.
4. Require the corpus to change the working model before synthesis.
5. Only then write, plan, critique, design, or execute.

Naming a corpus without retrieving and transferring a mechanism is a failed pass.

## Routing

### Fiction / prose / story / character / anthology
Prefer Story Brainstorm when it contains enough accepted cards for the task. Story Brainstorm is a rights-verified narrative primitive corpus; use it for pacing, withholding, dialogue mechanics, social friction, bodily observation, procedural danger, point of view, and compression.

When Story Brainstorm is not yet populated enough, use the rights-verified literary feeder corpus in Vernepunk while preserving Story Brainstorm evidence discipline.

For the active Omnitread fiction lane, Jules Verne and H. G. Wells are designated donors. Their use is mandatory unless Drew explicitly overrides it. Do not imitate their sentences. Mine actual source behavior or validated corpus cards and transplant function.

Verne transfer candidates include operational dialogue, machinery/procedure as suspense, low-information reporting, resource/endurance pressure, material causality, and consequence moving through systems.

Wells transfer candidates include ordinary social texture colliding with the impossible, isolation as loss of feedback, hidden agency, classification failure, delayed recognition, embodied awkwardness, and incompatible ontologies left active.

Native project corpora such as Omnitread should also be used when they govern tone, causality, source style, or story-engine behavior.

### Systems / game mechanics / product synthesis
Prefer Thunder Brainstorm. Retrieve indexed mechanic references, source packets, pattern cards, session learnings, and prior project evidence before inventing from scratch.

### Mixed commissions
Use the smallest corpus set that covers materially different axes. Do not concatenate corpora for ceremony.

### Future brainstorm corpora
Any later corpus promoted by Drew enters this resolver automatically. Select it when it is the smallest correct source for the commission.

## Fiction preflight
Before prose:
- lock canon, branch status, and protected unknowns;
- recover at least one applicable donor primitive/mechanism;
- surface at least one contradiction, alternate route, or rejected easy answer from corpus comparison;
- when simulation is requested or implied, load Fictional Discovery Simulation actor state and judgment machinery;
- do not preload a moral/theme target as plot.

If corpus retrieval produces no non-obvious structural change, run another pass. Decorative citation is not use.

## Weak-draft gate
When work has good bones but does not land:
1. Do not polish it.
2. Retrieve successful corpus analogues.
3. She-Raw gets the pickaxe: attack the easiest story, hidden assumption, and attractive simplification.
4. Tetsuya gets the shovel: remove non-load-bearing material and preserve the working structure.
5. If the beat is a non-event, retrieve the NO INCIDENT / Steady Hand lineage.
6. Re-run from the last trusted state rather than line-editing failed delivery.

## NO INCIDENT transfer
For a non-event, ask:
- What consequential thing could happen but does not?
- What live mechanism makes the non-event a choice rather than emptiness?
- What causal position or geometry carries character without biography?
- What visible body fact changes stakes without deciding intent?
- Where should the cut occur before classification?

Transfer the organ, not the train/lever props.

## Rights and provenance
When using public-domain or rights-cleared literary sources:
- preserve source identity and provenance;
- quote minimally; prefer mechanism extraction;
- separate source observation from generated inference;
- never counterfeit an author's style;
- prefer the source moment that solves the mechanism, not merely the most famous source.

## Failure conditions
Reject a pass when:
- the same output would have been produced without corpus retrieval;
- the corpus is named but not consulted;
- prose/design is produced first and corpus justification pasted afterward;
- a source becomes decorative analogy;
- generic assistant cadence overrides available donor mechanics;
- a designated donor such as Verne or Wells is silently omitted;
- one tidy lesson erases a contradiction exposed by the corpus.

## Acceptance chain
A valid pass has an internal evidence chain:

**CORPUS USED -> EVIDENCE RETRIEVED -> MECHANISM TRANSFERRED -> OUTPUT CHANGED.**

Expose the chain when Drew asks for receipts or when it materially explains a consequential choice.

## Compact law

**Clock first. Corpus before composition. Both are default.**
