I

THE LOAD-BEARING LAW

Beauty is free.
Contradiction is not.

A decisive ASCII contact frame owns the semantic sentence: support, contact, root, load, facing, custody, and consequence. The finished animation can become lavish, anatomical, fleshy, graphic, painterly, skeletal, or impossible. It still has to mean the same thing.

Beauty may decorate Kinesis. Beauty may exaggerate Kinesis. Beauty may not contradict Kinesis.
