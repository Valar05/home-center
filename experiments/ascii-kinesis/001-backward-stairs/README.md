# ASCII Kinesis 001 — Backward Stairs / Diagonal Hip Shift

## Observation

A user-supplied live movement clip showed backward descent of a short rail-less stair, a turn at the bottom, and backward ascent. During ascent, the performer discovered that a diagonal pelvis/hip shift made the movement substantially easier than attempting a naive straight-backward rise.

The source video remains a conversation upload and is not committed here. This directory persists the derived motion abstraction, structured motion data, compiled ASCII video, self-contained SPA, reconstruction source, and provenance boundary.

## What this entry proves

It proves that this specific specimen can be represented as literal ASCII source/contact poses and compiled into a coherent motion artifact while preserving the observed distinction between descent and ascent.

It does NOT prove that backward stair travel is generally safe, that this is the final movement form, or that the candidate Embodied Compression interpretation is universal.

## Generated derivative

`build.py` deterministically emits `contact-sheet.txt` and rebuilds the self-contained SPA/video from the tracked structured source. The same contact-sheet text is embedded literally and selectable inside `backward-stairs.motion.html`; it is therefore durable in the tracked artifact even when the convenience `.txt` derivative is regenerated.
