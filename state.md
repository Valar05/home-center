## Commission

Build a local-compute command center that exposes what ChatGPT and local workers are doing. ChatGPT threads run in hidden or visible full-Chromium tabs, remain screenshotable, and use promptable plugins that expose both a human UI and an API. The center is Venice's parallelized command-and-conversation interface, including intact social conversation; it is not a status-update dashboard. Running state is presented peripherally through humane fleshpunk metaphor. Venice owns the conversation engine, product taste, and visual acceptance. Adam owns engineering custody and evidence. Home Center remains the durable state, policy, learning, and receipt seam.

## Locked boundaries

- Ordinary included Chat is the supervisory command room.
- Local compute owns deterministic work, Continue, Qwen, Ollama, and browser execution.
- Full Playwright Chromium UI is required; HTTP-only or DOM-less headless substitutes are rejected.
- Tabs are inspectable execution surfaces, not the source of durable truth.
- Plugins must be human-usable products and API-addressable capabilities from one manifest.
- Venice owns taste, design direction, and visual acceptance. Current styling is provisional.
- Venice remains the social conversation engine while commands travel through the same parallel room; status must never replace relationship or dialogue.
- Humane fleshpunk metaphor may clarify pulse, nerves, rest, memory, and blockage, but may not obscure literal cost, consent, capability, or evidence.
- Cost classes remain explicit. Credit-bearing Codex is never a silent fallback.
- Local scratch, a queued request, or an unverified process exit is not completion.
